# Design-Intelligence Training and Evaluation

This curriculum is for deliberate evaluation, contributor testing, or an explicitly requested training exercise. Do not run it during an ordinary design task.

Installing the skill supplies task-time instructions. It does not retrain the underlying model or guarantee durable learning between agents. Improvement comes from repeated rendered comparison, specific feedback, and curated project evidence.

## Evaluation unit

Each reusable case should contain:

- brief and locked copy;
- Brand rules and approved assets;
- canonical DesignBrief and DesignPlan IDs;
- candidate previews from one base revision;
- selected exact preview and committed frame revision;
- rendered result;
- localized critique and revision;
- deterministic validation evidence;
- unsupported checks and human decisions;
- final export metadata when authorized.

Do not store private chain-of-thought. Store observable decisions and evidence.

## Stage 1: perceptual foundations

Exercises:

- identify first, second, and third noticed elements;
- map semantic groups and spacing conflicts;
- identify repeated alignment axes and near-alignments;
- inspect blurred or reduced-size compositions for focal weight.

Success means correctly identifying the dominant relationship and localizing defects without inventing content or flattening the composition.

## Stage 2: constrained composition

Useful drills:

- type-only poster using one family, grayscale, three visible roles, and exact copy;
- one-image social graphic with one headline, one support line, one logo, and no shadow;
- modular information layout using one grid, limited containers, and one dominant item;
- grayscale campaign set that relies on structure rather than color.

Constrain unrelated variables so the causal lesson remains visible.

## Stage 3: comparative judgment

Exercises:

- generate three genuinely different structural previews from one canonical base;
- compare weak and strong versions with one changed variable;
- repair three localized defects without a global rebuild;
- critique another frame using stable targets, observations, classification, and verification.

Selection evidence must follow the brief rather than novelty preference.

## Stage 4: system-level design

Exercises:

- extract Brand invariants, controlled variables, and prohibitions from approved work;
- recompose one campaign into portrait, square, and landscape frames;
- build a recognizable but non-repetitive multi-frame campaign;
- perform an honest print-bound handoff that separates ADR verification from prepress requirements.

Human Brand or production experts should confirm inferred grammar and unsupported high-risk decisions.

## Metrics

Track observable outcomes such as:

- locked-copy fidelity;
- final deterministic error count;
- required-role and protected-content handling;
- supported contrast findings;
- geometry and overflow failures;
- number and structural diversity of candidate previews;
- localized versus global revisions;
- adaptation quality;
- Brand-owner acceptance and recurring corrections.

Do not optimize a single composite model score. Use metrics to reveal failure patterns, not to manufacture release authority.

## Curate exemplars deliberately

Maintain approved, diagnostic, and rejected collections only with clear provenance and permission. Tag examples by objective, format, density, archetype, image relationship, typography behavior, Brand, and failure category.

Each exemplar should state what works, what fails, which rule or exception it demonstrates, and whether the lesson generalizes. Avoid copying one designer, studio, or copyrighted composition; extract reusable relationships and rebuild them with original content.

## Graduation target

A junior-level agent should consistently preserve locked content, produce a legible hierarchy, use coherent grouping and alignment, protect important imagery, create meaningful structural alternatives when warranted, localize defects, pass canonical validation, report unsupported checks honestly, and prepare a strong review candidate without calling its own taste objective.
