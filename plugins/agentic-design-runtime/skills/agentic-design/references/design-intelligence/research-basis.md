# Research Basis

This maintenance-only reference records the evidence categories behind the curriculum. Verify current specifications before updating claims; research and platform guidance can change.

## Codex instruction architecture

- OpenAI, AGENTS.md: <https://developers.openai.com/codex/agent-configuration/agents-md>
- OpenAI, Agent Skills: <https://developers.openai.com/codex/build-skills>
- OpenAI, customization overview: <https://developers.openai.com/codex/customization/overview>

Practical implication: keep persistent guidance compact, route task-specific depth through references, and use executable checks only for conditions that can be measured truthfully.

## Visual design and cognition

- Nielsen Norman Group, visual-design principles: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/principles-visual-design/>
- Nielsen Norman Group, visual hierarchy: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/>
- Nielsen Norman Group, text over images: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/text-over-images/>
- Nielsen Norman Group, cognitive load: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/minimize-cognitive-load/>
- Nielsen Norman Group, visual-design testing: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/testing-visual-design/>
- Nielsen Norman Group, Gestalt proximity: <https://www.nngroup.com/articles/gestalt-proximity/>
- Elliot and Maier, contextual color psychology review: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4383146/>

Practical implication: teach hierarchy, scale, balance, contrast, grouping, and context before stylistic recipes.

## AI layout research

- VASCAR, visual-aware self-correction: <https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04237v2>
- CAL-RAG, retrieval and localized layout feedback: <https://arxiv.org/html/2506.21934>
- PosterLlama, semantic content relationships: <https://arxiv.org/html/2404.00995v2>
- PosterO, structured layout trees: <https://arxiv.org/html/2505.07843v2>
- LayoutRectifier, local layout correction: <https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11177v2>
- Graphic Design with Large Multimodal Models: <https://arxiv.org/html/2404.14368v1>

Practical implication: semantic structure, multiple candidates, rendered feedback, localized critique, and iterative correction are more credible than one-pass coordinate or code generation.

## Limitation

These sources support workflow architecture, not one universal aesthetic formula. Human art direction, cultural context, Brand strategy, and specialist production knowledge remain necessary for consequential work.
